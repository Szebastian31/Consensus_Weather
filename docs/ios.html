Download .ipa

Unsigned build · iOS 16 or later · iPhone & iPad

Install with AltStore (recommended)

  1. Install AltStore on your Mac or PC, then AltServer.
  2. Plug in your iPhone, and in AltServer choose Install AltStore → your device.
  3. On the iPhone, download the .ipa above (tap and save to Files).
  4. Open AltStore → My Apps → +, pick the .ipa, and sign in with a free Apple ID.
  5. Trust it under Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, then launch.

Or use Sideloadly

  1. Install Sideloadly on your Mac or PC.
  2. Connect your iPhone, drag in the .ipa, enter your free Apple ID, and press Start.
  3. Trust the app under Settings → General → VPN & Device Management.
Heads up: apps signed with a free Apple ID stop opening after 7 days. Just re-install with AltStore or Sideloadly to refresh — your saved places and settings stay put. (A paid Apple Developer account extends this to a year and enables TestFlight.)
